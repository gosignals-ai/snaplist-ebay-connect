SNAPLIST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

SnapList is a private, single-user desktop application that helps its owner prepare and publish marketplace listings.

Information handled

The desktop application may process product photographs, listing information, and eBay authorization information solely to create and manage listings requested by the user.

Storage

SnapList settings and credentials are stored locally on the user’s computer. This website does not store passwords, secret keys, authorization codes, photographs, listings, buyer information, or order information.

Sharing

SnapList does not sell personal information. Information is sent to eBay only when necessary to perform an action approved by the user.

Authorization and deletion

The user may revoke SnapList’s access through their eBay account settings. Locally stored information can be removed by clearing the saved connection or uninstalling the desktop application.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to keep credentials local and limit information handling to functions requested by the user.
